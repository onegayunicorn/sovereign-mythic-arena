# Paean Mythic Stack Battle Royal rules

Five weighted axes: mythic coherence 20%, mathematics 25%, physics 25%, reproducibility 15%, craft 15%. Four hard gates: pinned clean build, unit/dimensional tests, headless no-network execution, and verifiable input/output provenance. A failed or unknown gate invalidates the bout regardless of score.

Results are bout-scoped, not general model rankings. Seed/demo scores are not measurements. Verified records require a real harness run, raw evidence, pinned model/toolchain digests, rubric version, and independently verifiable signatures. Physical targets (including the Chronos Pendulum) are proposals until measured.

Before high-impact publication, gate override, artifact signing, or release, use the independent buddy-check and nonce-bound reverse-handshake workflow in `paean/buddy-check/README.md`. Missing evidence or disagreement means pending/abstain, not pass. A hash/signature protects record integrity; it does not prove a physical experiment occurred.
