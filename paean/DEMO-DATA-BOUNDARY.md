# Demo data and ranking claim boundaries

Keep three signals distinct: (1) a bout winner under a named rubric and evidence set; (2) this installation’s local crowd vote or Paean tournament standings; (3) an external Arena category ranking with a dated source snapshot. Never infer overall “#1” from one bout, a crowd vote, or seed data.

The supplied tallyboard’s seeded scores, deterministic jitter, and static passing-gate badges are illustrative demo data, not physical measurements or verified harness results. Mark them `demo-seed` and exclude them from verified standings. Verified entries require actual harness outputs, gate evidence, pinned digests, provenance, and valid signatures.

For claims such as “first place,” include scope, category, scoring method, sample count, uncertainty, timestamp, and a source or verifiable local ledger reference. External leaderboard snapshots are freshness-sensitive and must be rechecked before release.
